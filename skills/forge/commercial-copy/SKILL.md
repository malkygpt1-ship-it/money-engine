# Commercial Copy

## Product naming

Use specific outcome-oriented names. Prefer `Monthly Budget Planner Toolkit` over vague names such as `Ultimate Money System`.

## Promise

State the useful outcome and mechanism without guaranteed financial, health, legal or performance claims.

Good pattern: `Organise income, bills and savings in one monthly workbook with automatic totals and variance tracking.`

## Onboarding

The first screen/page/sheet should answer:
- what is this;
- who is it for;
- what do I do first;
- what can I safely edit;
- what result should I expect;
- where do I go if something looks wrong.

## Tone

- concise and practical;
- no AI self-reference;
- no fake scarcity;
- no invented testimonials;
- no unexplained jargon;
- no filler designed only to increase page count.

## Examples

Use realistic but clearly illustrative example data. Never present invented figures as customer results or evidence.
